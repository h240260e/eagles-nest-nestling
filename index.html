About Eagles Nest Nestling

Eagles Nest Nestling is a small private infant school located in Magunje Township. We provide quality education for young learners from ECD to Grade 4 in a caring, safe and supportive environment.

Our goal is to give every child a strong foundation for their educational journey while helping them develop confidence, good character and a love for learning.
